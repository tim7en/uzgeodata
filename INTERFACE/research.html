RESEARCH FOUNDATIONS

Published research behind
every layer of the atlas.

The datasets, methods and reported records this public preview is built from, bound to the four scientific layers and to a role in verification or validation. Listing a paper means we implement or ingest it -- it does not mean our implementation has passed independent scientific reproduction.

01 Reference ↓02 Observations ↓03 Analysis ↓04 Models ↓Validation targets ↓

How verification and validation are used here

Computational supportInputs hashed, units checked, coverage retained. Establishes that a number was computed as declared -- not that it is accurate.
Held-out evaluationModels scored on periods excluded from fitting, against a seasonal-climatology benchmark, with the split published beside the score.
Independent reproductionRecipes re-run against pinned inputs by someone other than the original author, compared to the published baseline. The gate no atlas attribute has passed yet.

The registry behind this page is published as publications.json and carries the same layer, role and status fields the page renders.

LAYER 2 · DATED OBSERVATIONS AND EVIDENCE

Dated observations and evidence

What was recorded or estimated here during this period. Verification here means unit, coverage and provenance checks on ingestion -- numerical support, not measurement accuracy.

Abatzoglou, J. T., Dobrowski, S. Z., Parks, S. A., Hegewisch, K. C. (2018). TerraClimate, a high-resolution global dataset of monthly climate and climatic water balance from 1958-2015. Scientific Data, 5, 170191. Open online ↗

input datain use

Monthly precipitation, evapotranspiration, soil moisture, runoff, snow-water equivalent, deficit index, temperatures and vapour-pressure deficit, area-reduced to basins with the resolution gate. Its water-balance model structure is inherited by every TerraClimate-derived series.

Used by: history series, cube, trends.html, water-flow.html.

Muñoz-Sabater, J., et al. (2021). ERA5-Land: a state-of-the-art global reanalysis dataset for land surface applications. Earth System Science Data, 13, 4349-4383. Open online ↗

input datain use

Runoff and mean temperature, withheld at level 12 (1.05 cells per median basin) and analysed at level 7 only, where they show no surviving trend.

Used by: history series, trends.html (level 7).

Hall, D. K., Riggs, G. A. (2021). MODIS/Aqua Snow Cover Daily L3 Global 500m SIN Grid (MYD10A1), version 61. NASA NSIDC DAAC. Open online ↗

input datain use

Observed snow cover, the one non-modelled series in the record. Withdrawn from trend use: missing months increase across the record and the cause is unresolved.

Used by: history series (snw), trends.html (reported, not promoted).

Scientific Information Center, Interstate Commission for Water Coordination (2024). Water Yearbooks of the Aral Sea basin, 2022-2024, section 2 (withdrawals, reservoirs, environmental deliveries). SIC ICWC, Tashkent. Open online ↗

reported recordreported record

Calendar-year country withdrawals, limits, reservoir inflow/release pairs and Aral/delta deliveries, with row-level citations. Management accounting at reach and country support, not gauges.

Used by: water-flow.html, water-flow sources register.

Ragettli, S., Kreiner, A., Yakovlev, A., Anarbekov, O., Al-Zu'bi, M., Urazkeldiyev, A., Siegfried, T. (2025). Unraveling agricultural water use in three Central Asian irrigation oases using remote sensing. Journal of Hydrology: Regional Studies, 59, 102414. Open online ↗

validation targetplanned integration

Remote-sensing quantification of agricultural water use in the Bukhara, Samarkand and Kashkadarya oases of Uzbekistan, 2017-2022. Exactly the class of external evidence the canal-withdrawal gap calls for: observed consumptive use in the irrigated alluvium against which the managed term missing from our gridded record can be bounded.

Used by: planned managed-water layer, water-flow.html limitations.

Marti, B., et al. (2023). CA-discharge: Geo-located discharge time series for mountainous rivers in Central Asia. Scientific Data, 10, 579 (295 gauge locations compiled from national hydrometeorological yearbooks; monthly, 10-day and daily series). Open online ↗

validation targetplanned integration

The region's compiled gauge-discharge record: the designated held-out target for the Pskem/Chirchik model line beyond its current station records, and the anchor series for testing precipitation forcings region-wide.

Used by: discharge modelling protocol, planned regional forcing tests.

De Keyser, J., et al. (2023). Integrating Open-Source Datasets to Analyze the Transboundary Water-Food-Energy-Climate Nexus in Central Asia. Water, 15(19), 3482. Open online ↗

verification referenceplanned integration

A compiled inventory of open datasets for the region; a cross-check that this project's coverage ledger has not missed an open source, and a partner catalogue for the thematic-atlas modules.

Used by: catalogue coverage review, data-groups.json.

LAYER 3 · ANALYTICAL PRODUCTS

Analytical products

How the record compares, varies or changes. Verification here means the analysis code is tested against reference implementations and every result carries its period, baseline and corrections.

Mann, H. B. (1945). Nonparametric tests against trend. Econometrica, 13(3), 245-259.

method sourcemethod implemented

The rank trend test behind every basin verdict; verified against scipy.stats.kendalltau to 1e-9.

Used by: trends study, TESTS/test_trends.py.

Sen, P. K. (1968). Estimates of the regression coefficient based on Kendall's tau. Journal of the American Statistical Association, 63(324), 1379-1389.

method sourcemethod implemented

Median-of-pairwise-slopes magnitude estimate reported beside every verdict; verified against scipy.stats.theilslopes.

Used by: trends study, TESTS/test_trends.py.

Hamed, K. H., Rao, A. R. (1998). A modified Mann-Kendall trend test for autocorrelated data. Journal of Hydrology, 204(1-4), 182-196.

method sourcemethod implemented

Variance inflation for serial persistence, applied by default with the uncorrected verdict retained beside it.

Used by: trends study.

Benjamini, Y., Hochberg, Y. (1995). Controlling the false discovery rate: a practical and powerful approach to multiple testing. Journal of the Royal Statistical Society B, 57(1), 289-300.

method sourcemethod implemented

Family control within each variable across thousands of simultaneous basin tests; removes every significant basin for five of twelve testable variables.

Used by: trends study.

Marti, B., Siegfried, T. (2023). riversCentralAsia: An R package to support data pre- and postprocessing for hydrological modelling with RS MINERVE. Journal of Open Source Software, 8(82), 4805. Open online ↗

method sourceplanned integration

Open-source regional tooling for hydrometeorological data preparation and model pre/post-processing; a candidate cross-check for this project's Python aggregation and model workflows rather than a replacement of them.

Used by: model workflow review.
