6 · Method

How the numbers are made

Source

TerraClimate v1.1 monthly precipitation, PDSI, runoff and PET from the producer's yearly NetCDF files (June 2026 release). Each 1/24° grid is reduced to all 7,445 level-12 basins by fractional cell overlap, then area-weighted into 438 level-7 units, two systems and Uzbekistan's 14 regions.

Norms

  • 1991–2020: the current WMO standard normal.
  • 1961–1990: the previous standard normal.
  • Trailing 10, 20 and 30 years: the mean of the water years just before the year shown. They start in 1971, 1981 and 1991.

Indices

SPI-12 fits a gamma distribution to each unit's own 1991–2020 water-year totals. PDSI is the water-year mean of TerraClimate's monthly Palmer index. It already expresses departure from local normal, so its difference from a norm shows change in drought state.

Limits

    Built by PIPELINES/build_drought_study.py. Downloads: all 7,445 basins × water years (Parquet) · 438 sub-basins (Parquet) · Uzbekistan regions (Parquet) · summary (JSON). Each basin's own record is in the Drought tab of the basin explorer, with a CSV download.
