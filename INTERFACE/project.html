THE PROJECT

A shared view of
connected water systems.

UzGeoData is a basin atlas for researchers investigating the Amu Darya and Syr Darya systems, including the headwaters beyond Uzbekistan.

It connects published HydroATLAS characteristics with independent estimates from open environmental data. Explore a catchment, inspect its monthly record, and take the values and their provenance into your own analysis.

NEXT RESEARCH PRIORITIES

Verify & strengthen

Independently reproduce attributes, investigate missing snow observations, review source coverage, and extend attribute families.

LONGER-TERM DIRECTION

Extend & connect

Versioned atlas editions, carefully assessed historical extensions, a national environmental atlas, and a monitoring ontology.

Research milestones depend on evidence and review. They are not release commitments; the broader 2000–2026 ambition is separate from the published 2003–2024 monthly preview.

Scientific status, citation & reuse →

This is a public research preview. No atlas attribute has passed independent scientific reproduction. The portal does not provide operational forecasts, engineering design flows, or allocation advice.

Coverage varies. A missing value is not zero. Sources may differ in period, resolution, and method. Snow remains available for inspection but is withdrawn from trend analysis because increasing missingness is unresolved.

Keep the metadata with your downloads and cite original datasets. Each source retains its own reuse terms.
