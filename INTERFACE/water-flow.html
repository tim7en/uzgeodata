CASE STUDY · REGIONAL WATER BALANCE

Where the region’s water goes
and how much of that we actually know.

A water flow diagram for the Amu Darya and Syr Darya, adapted from the Water Flow Diagram method. Its published applications are municipal, where one utility can account for what it abstracted, delivered and lost. Nothing at that resolution is published for this region — so this is drawn at basin-system scale, and every flow states how it is known.

Colour is provenance, not water type

The published applications of this method colour flows by what kind of water they carry. Here colour says how a flow is known, because at this scale that is the distinction a reader most needs and least often gets from a Sankey diagram.

MeasuredThis project's observation record or plant inventory. Reproducible from the published release.
AdministeredPublished by the bodies that allocate the water. What was allocated and diverted, not an independent measurement of what flowed.

This diagram does not close

This diagram does not close, and is not made to. Precipitation less evapotranspiration leaves a surplus larger than the recorded diversion, while the runoff series exceeds that surplus outright: two models on different grids disagree, and forcing the arrows to balance would hide exactly the thing worth seeing.

Precipitation less evapotranspiration leaves 93.3 km³/yr, against 58.1 km³/yr recorded as diverted in 2022. The remaining 35.2 km³/yr is drawn as not accounted for and is not spare water: it includes river outflow, wetland and delta losses, aquifer recharge, and the disagreement between two models on different grids.

Why it is not drawn per basin

Basin systems, not municipalities. Withdrawal here is administered by country and canal system while the record is organised by catchment, and canal delivery crosses basin divides, so a national figure cannot be honestly disaggregated to a basin.

The measured fluxes are 22-year means; the administered figures are the 2022 hydrological year. They are drawn together because that is what is published, and the mismatch is stated rather than hidden by averaging one to the other.

What the method expects and nobody publishes here

  • Distribution losses. Often the largest single finding in the method’s published applications. No baseline exists for this region — the 2023 national plan targets a 10 per cent reduction without stating what from.
  • Groundwater abstraction. The method separates surface from groundwater. National usable groundwater is given as 0.5 km³/yr against an estimated 27.6 km³/yr yield; abstraction is not published at a usable scale.
  • Untreated wastewater. We hold the volume 53 plants treat. What is generated and never reaches a plant is the quantity the method most wants.

These are drawn as dashed hairlines at fixed width rather than omitted, so their absence is visible and can never be read as small.

Sources

  • Interstate Commission for Water Coordination, Basin Water Organizations — BWO Amu Darya and BWO Syr Darya are the inter-ministerial bodies that set and record withdrawal limits and deliveries for the two basins. They are the administering authority, so their figures are what was allocated and diverted, not an independent measurement of what flowed.
  • CAWater-Info Yearbook 2022, Section 2: Water-related situation in the Aral Sea basin — Basin and country diversion figures for the 2022 hydrological year.
  • CAWater-Info Yearbook 2023, Section 5: Key water developments, Uzbekistan — National totals by source and the sectoral shares.
  • SIC ICWC analytical report 2023 — Context for reservoir inflow and basin conditions; not the source of any figure recorded here.
  • UzGeoData observation record — Precipitation, evapotranspiration and runoff, monthly for 7,445 level-12 basins, 2003-2024. Converted to volumes using published basin areas.
  • UzGeoData wastewater plant inventory — 53 plants with discharge in cubic metres per day, population served, treatment level and outfall basin.

Reuse not yet established. The reuse terms of the ICWC and SIC ICWC pages have not been established. The figures are reproduced here as published statistics with their source and retrieval date, which is how a citation works and is not the same as a licence. Before this diagram is presented as a product rather than a working case study, those terms need checking with SIC ICWC, and this field replaced with what they say.

Method reference. A methodology for integrated water balancing that connects WASH, IWRM and the human right to water and sanitation, visualising abstraction, distribution, consumption, wastewater and losses for a municipality so stakeholders can see where water goes. Developed with case studies including Bern and St Gallen (Switzerland), Tarapoto (Peru), Agadir (Morocco), Pespire (Honduras) and Santa Maria Bulacan (Philippines). — published applications.
